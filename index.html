From request to receipt

  1. Received

    The request arrives from a platform, a partner or a client and is read at once.

  2. Confirmed

    Details are checked and the booking is confirmed in writing.

  3. Assigned

    A driver and car, or a residence, are assigned and the guest is told who to expect.

    The flight and the journey are followed until the guest is at the door.

  5. Completed

    The service is closed and anything unusual is recorded.

  6. Invoiced

    An itemised invoice goes out in the agreed currency.

Statement of the Company

Horus X Ltd, company number 16483511

  1. The Company

    Horus X Ltd is a private company limited by shares, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 16483511.

  2. Principal activities

    The Company arranges ground transport, chauffeured travel and serviced accommodation for travel platforms, travel agencies, companies and private clients.

  3. Engagement

    Each engagement is governed by written terms agreed with the client. The client's agreement is with the Company, which answers to the client for the services it arranges.

  4. Suppliers

    Services are performed by the Company and by suppliers it appoints, who are held to the Company's standards.

  5. Confidentiality

    Information about guests is used only to deliver the services requested and is disclosed only to those who need it for that purpose.

    The Company's terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales.
